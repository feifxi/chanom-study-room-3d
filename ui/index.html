Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Rooms, voice, chat and activities are free for up to 25 people at once. Plus gives the rooms you run more space and includes premium cosmetics.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Connecta runs in your browser on a laptop or phone. Friends join from a link.

Can my friends join without an account?

Yes. They can join Common rooms and rooms whose owner allows guests. They choose a name and a look before walking in.

Who can talk to me?

You can voice chat with people nearby in rooms that support voice. Only friends can DM you. You can also report or block people.

How old do I need to be?

You must be 13 or older.
